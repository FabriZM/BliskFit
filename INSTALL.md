# Installing BliskFit

BliskFit is a gym tracker that keeps everything on your phone. There's no account and nothing is uploaded. There are two ways to get it; both are free.

## Option 1: Android app (recommended)

Rest-timer alerts arrive on time even with the screen locked, and the app tells you when an update is out.

1. On your phone, open **https://github.com/FabriZM/BliskFit/releases/latest** and tap the `.apk` file under **Assets** to download it.
2. Open the downloaded file. Android will say it can't install apps from this source: tap **Settings**, turn on **Allow from this source** for your browser, go back and tap **Install**.
3. Open BliskFit. When it asks to send notifications, allow it (needed for the rest timer and reminders).

**Updates:** when a new version is out, a bar at the top of the app offers it (or check in Profile → ⚙ → App updates). Tap **Update**: the app saves a backup first, then downloads the new version, and Android asks you to confirm. Your workouts stay.

**Prefer automatic updates?** Install [Obtainium](https://github.com/ImranR98/Obtainium), add the app with the URL `https://github.com/FabriZM/BliskFit`, and it installs BliskFit and keeps it up to date.

## Option 2: Web app (any phone)

1. Open **https://fabrizm.github.io/BliskFit/** in Chrome (Android) or Safari (iPhone).
2. Install it: in Chrome, menu ⋮ → **Install app** (or **Add to home screen**); in Safari, Share → **Add to Home Screen**.

It works offline and updates itself (tap **Reload** on the "Update available" bar). Rest-timer alerts only work while the app is open. Browsers can clear a site's data when the phone runs low on space, so save backups now and then.

## Keep a backup

Everything lives only on your phone. Save a backup now and then from Profile → ⚙ → **Backup and data** → **Save backup**, and keep the file somewhere else (Google Drive, your computer). On a new phone, install BliskFit and use **Restore** with that file.

## Switching from the web app to the Android app

They don't share data. In the web app save a backup, then in the Android app restore it.
