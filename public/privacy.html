BliskFit privacy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

BliskFit is a personal gym tracker. It has no accounts and no servers of its own, and it doesn't collect, sell or share any data.

Your data stays on your phone

Workouts, routines, exercises, body measurements, progress photos and settings are stored only on your device, in the app's own storage. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose to.

Google Drive backups (optional)

  • If you connect Google Drive, BliskFit saves backup files of your data to a "BliskFit backups" folder in your own Drive, and reads them back when you restore.
  • It asks only for the drive.file permission, which lets it see and change only the files it created. It cannot see anything else in your Drive.
  • The backups go straight from your phone to Google. The developer never receives them, your Google account details or your sign-in.
  • You can disconnect at any time in Settings → Backup and data, or remove access from your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Backups already in your Drive stay there until you delete them.
  • BliskFit's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Things you choose to share

Backup files, CSV exports, shared routines and workout images are created on your phone and go only where you send them.

Update checks

The Android app checks GitHub once a day for a newer version. That request contains no personal data.
